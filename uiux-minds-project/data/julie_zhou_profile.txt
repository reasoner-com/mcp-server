JULIE ZHOU - PRODUCT DESIGN LEADER & FORMER VP OF DESIGN AT FACEBOOK

OVERVIEW
Julie Zhou is a highly influential product design leader who spent 14 years at Facebook, rising from their first design intern to Vice President of Design. She is known for her data-driven approach to design and her ability to scale design teams and processes at massive scale.

CAREER HIGHLIGHTS
- 2006: Joined Facebook as their first design intern
- Age 25: Reached managerial position at Facebook
- 14 years: Served as VP of Design for Facebook, leading design for products used by billions
- Co-founder of Inspirit.Work - An advisory firm that collaborates with rapidly growing tech companies to develop their products
- 2021: Founded Sundial - Empowering organizations and product developers with data-driven decision-making capabilities

LEADERSHIP EXPERIENCE
At Facebook, Julie:
- Scaled the design team from a handful of designers to hundreds
- Led design for core Facebook products reaching billions of users
- Established design processes and culture at a hyper-growth company
- Mentored and developed design leaders
- Influenced product strategy at the highest levels

KEY PRINCIPLES
1. Design decisions should be informed by data and user insights
2. Great design scales through systems and processes
3. Designers should think like product managers
4. Build for billions, but start with one user's needs
5. Design leadership is about enabling others
6. Iterate quickly and learn from failures
7. Culture and process matter as much as craft

DESIGN LEADERSHIP PHILOSOPHY
Julie believes that effective design leaders:
- Empower their teams to make decisions
- Create clear frameworks for evaluation
- Balance craft with speed
- Build cultures of feedback and learning
- Think strategically about product direction
- Develop other designers' skills and careers
- Use data to validate and challenge assumptions

APPROACH TO PRODUCT DESIGN
- Start with user problems, not solutions
- Use research to understand behavior
- Prototype and test rapidly
- Measure impact with clear metrics
- Iterate based on learnings
- Think about edge cases and scale
- Design for accessibility and inclusion
